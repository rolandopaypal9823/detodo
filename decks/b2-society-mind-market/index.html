Target lexicon

History & Human Heritage

Key words

  • Epoch noun — A particular period of time in history marked by notable events.
  • Predecessor noun — Something or someone that came before another.
  • Legacy noun — Long-lasting impact or achievements handed down from the past.
  • Precedent noun — An earlier event or action that serves as an example.

Discussion prompts

  • Do you agree that “history repeats itself”, or do modern technological advancements make our epoch completely unique?
  • Which historical figure left the most important legacy for modern society? Why?
  • How can learning about historical economic mistakes help us avoid crises today?

02

Economic vocabulary

Economy & Financial Reality

Key words

  • Purchasing power noun — The financial ability to buy products and services.
  • Inflation noun — A general increase in prices and a fall in the purchasing value of money.
  • Commodity noun — A raw material or primary agricultural product that can be bought and sold.
  • Scarcity noun — The state of being in short supply or limited availability.

Speaking task

How has inflation impacted consumer behavior in your country over the last three years?

Push yourself

Use at least two key words from this slide in your answer, and give one concrete example — a price, a product, a habit that changed.

03

Key concept · Consumer persuasion

The Power of Marketing

Target lexicon

  • Brand loyalty noun — The tendency of consumers to continuously purchase one brand over another.
  • Resonate verb — To evoke a positive feeling or emotional response in an audience.
  • Demographic noun — A specific section of a population grouped by age, income or interest.

Question

What is a recent marketing campaign that deeply resonated with you, and why was it effective?

Follow-up

Which demographic was that campaign aimed at? Would it work the same way with your parents’ generation?

Three concepts, three debates

Social Media & Digital Culture

The algorithm noun

Calculated rules that determine what content appears on your screen.

Does content curation restrict free thought?

Echo chambers noun

Environments where a person only encounters beliefs similar to their own.

How do echo chambers shape political views?

Monetization noun

Converting social media engagement and traffic into financial profit.

Are influencers trustworthy business actors?

05

Cultural lexicon

Faith, Religion & Society

Key words

  • Secular adjective — Denoting attitudes or activities with no religious or spiritual basis.
  • Heritage noun — Features belonging to the culture or traditions of a society, created in the past.
  • Devotion noun — Love, loyalty or enthusiasm for a cause or religious practice.
  • Morality noun — Principles concerning the distinction between right and wrong.

Discussion

Is modern society becoming increasingly secular? What role do traditional holidays play today?

Follow-up

Is a tradition still heritage if people keep the celebration but forget its meaning?

Individualism vs. collectivism

  • Individualism noun — Prioritizing personal independence, self-reliance and individual rights.
  • Collectivism noun — Emphasizing community harmony, family cohesion and shared goals.

Discussion

Which of these two value systems dominates your culture, and what are its main advantages?

Vocabulary spotlight · B2–C1 glossary

Four words to take away

Paradigm shift noun

A fundamental change in approach or underlying assumptions.

“Remote work caused a massive paradigm shift in the economy.”

Saturated adjective

Holding as much as can be absorbed; completely full.

“The smartphone market is heavily saturated with competing brands.”

Consumerism noun

A preoccupation with buying and owning consumer goods.

“Modern holidays are often criticized for excessive consumerism.”

Civic duty noun

A legal or moral obligation to participate in community decisions.

“Voting in national elections is viewed as an essential civic duty.”
